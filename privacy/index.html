Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

GOODVI is an iOS application for Scripture reading, prayer, daily virtues and personal reflection. This Privacy Policy describes the information handled when you use GOODVI. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean Arif Sadykov, the individual operator of GOODVI, based in Serbia. Questions or requests can be sent to arifsadykov7@gmail.com.

You can use GOODVI’s introductory screens without creating an account or providing an email address.

1. Information stored on your device

GOODVI keeps your onboarding answers and progress on your device to recommend a virtue and let you resume the introduction. When you use the relevant features, the app also keeps your journal entries, custom reflection choices, daily virtue selection, selected struggles, reading progress, completed chapters, preferences, reminders, favorite verses and prayers, and related app state on your device.

GOODVI has no account or cloud service for syncing your journal. Apple may include local app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

The Bible and daily reading content are included with the application. The app does not need your journal text to display the Bible.

2. Subscriptions and technical information

Subscription purchases and purchase restoration are currently unavailable. When subscriptions are available, GOODVI plans to use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit for purchases and Adapty to help check and manage subscription access.

The planned subscription integration can use an Adapty-generated profile identifier without a GOODVI account. If you make a subscription purchase through the App Store, Apple handles your Apple Account, billing and payment information under its own terms and privacy practices.

See Adapty's end-user privacy information and Apple's App Store privacy information.

3. Selected photos and local notifications

If you choose a photo as the background for a Scripture wallpaper, the app receives the item you select through Apple's photo picker and processes it on your device to compose the image. The generated wallpaper can be saved to your photo library using the permission you grant. Any separate sharing that you choose is handled by the destination you select and its own privacy terms.

GOODVI can request notification permission for optional reminders. Reminder preferences and scheduled local notifications are managed on your device. You can change or disable notifications in GOODVI and iOS Settings.

4. Journal exports, deletion and retention

When your journal is accessible, choosing Export entries in My data creates a temporary JSON file containing all saved reflection entries, including the choices recorded with them. Use the iOS share sheet to choose where to share or save the file. The destination handles its own saved copy. GOODVI cannot delete copies you have saved or shared outside its app container.

GOODVI attempts to remove the temporary file after iOS reports that sharing finished or was cancelled. If removal fails or sharing is interrupted, a temporary copy may remain. Unused temporary copies older than 24 hours are eligible for cleanup on a later launch. This does not guarantee secure erasure.

When your journal is accessible, you can edit individual reflection entries. The Delete entries action in My data removes all saved reflection entries after you confirm. Custom reflection choices and other app data are stored separately. Use iOS’s Delete App option to remove the app and its local data. Offload App retains documents and data. Manage backups and exported copies separately.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

For requests concerning information we handle, email arifsadykov7@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

We may need to verify a request before taking action. Please avoid including account passwords, one-time codes, card details or private journal text in your request.

6. Security, providers and processing locations

Local data is protected by the security features of iOS, subject to your device and backup settings. No application or service can guarantee absolute security.

We operate from Serbia. Google (Gmail) provides email, and GitHub hosts this site. These services may process information in other countries. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond, investigate an issue or process a request. Retention of provider logs and backups depends on the relevant service and settings.

This informational website does not use our own analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical request data under its privacy statement.

7. Children, changes and contact

GOODVI is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If an underage person uses GOODVI, their use and any purchase should comply with applicable law, Apple's rules and any necessary parent or guardian authorization. We do not knowingly operate a public profile or social-sharing feature for children.

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest published version. Material changes will be addressed with any notice required by law.
