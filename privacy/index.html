Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

GOODVI is an iOS application for Scripture reading, prayer, daily virtues and personal reflection. This Privacy Policy describes the information handled when you use GOODVI. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean Arif Sadykov, the individual operator of GOODVI, based in Serbia. 1. Information stored on your device

GOODVI keeps your onboarding answers and progress on your device to recommend a virtue and let you resume the introduction. When you use the relevant features, the app also keeps your journal entries, custom reflection choices, daily virtue selection, selected struggles, reading progress, completed chapters, preferences, reminders, favorite verses and prayers, and related app state on your device.

GOODVI has no account or cloud service for syncing your journal. Apple may include local app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

The Bible and daily reading content are included with the application. GOODVI does not send your journal text, prayer favorites or onboarding answers to Adapty. If you export your journal, you choose where to save or share the copy. If you add the GOODVI widget, it can show your selected virtue and a related verse on your Home Screen.

2. Subscriptions and technical information

GOODVI uses Apple’s App Store and StoreKit for subscriptions. GOODVI uses Adapty to help check and manage subscription access through a technical profile. You do not need a GOODVI account.

After the introduction, GOODVI contacts Adapty to check subscription access and load the subscription screen. Adapty assigns the technical profile its own identifier and can receive information before you make a purchase. The profile can be associated with an installation identifier, Apple’s vendor identifier (IDFV) when available, and technical information such as the device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, language settings, time zone, user agent and App Store storefront. Adapty records which subscription screen variant it assigns to an installation. GOODVI’s current settings filter out the SDK’s separate app-open, onboarding-screen and system-log events before transmission. We use profile and subscription information to provide Premium and to understand subscription activity.

For purchases and renewals through the App Store, Apple handles your Apple Account, billing and payment information under its own terms and privacy practices. Adapty can receive StoreKit information such as the product and transaction identifiers, purchase and subscription status, and a receipt or signed transaction. Adapty can associate this information with the technical profile to verify purchases, restore access and analyze subscriptions.

GOODVI disables collection of Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) and the SDK’s separate IP address lookup. IP address collection is enabled in GOODVI’s Adapty service settings. Adapty receives requests from your IP address. It uses the address from the first request to estimate a country, saves that country on your technical profile and uses it for country-level subscription analytics. Your App Store storefront may be different.

On the first profile sync, the SDK may send Adapty an Apple AdServices token if Apple supplies one. With Apple Ads attribution enabled, Adapty can store the result with the profile, including available campaign or ad group identifiers, to measure installs attributed to GOODVI ads in the App Store.

See Adapty's end-user privacy information and Apple's App Store privacy information.

3. Selected photos and local notifications

If you choose a photo as the background for a Scripture wallpaper, the app receives the item you select through Apple's photo picker and processes it on your device to compose the image. The generated wallpaper can be saved to your photo library using the permission you grant. Any separate sharing that you choose is handled by the destination you select and its own privacy terms.

GOODVI can request notification permission for optional reminders. Reminder preferences and scheduled local notifications are managed on your device. You can change or disable notifications in GOODVI and iOS Settings.

4. Journal exports, deletion and retention

When your journal is accessible, choosing Export entries in My data creates a temporary JSON file containing all saved reflection entries, including the choices recorded with them. Use the iOS share sheet to choose where to share or save the file. The destination handles its own saved copy. GOODVI cannot delete copies you have saved or shared outside its app container.

GOODVI attempts to remove the temporary file after iOS reports that sharing finished or was cancelled. If removal fails or sharing is interrupted, a temporary copy may remain. Unused temporary copies older than 24 hours are eligible for cleanup on a later launch. This does not guarantee secure erasure.

When your journal is accessible, you can edit individual reflection entries. The Delete entries action in My data removes all saved reflection entries after you confirm. Custom reflection choices and other app data are stored separately. Use iOS’s Delete App option to remove the app and its local data. Offload App retains documents and data. Manage backups and exported copies separately.

Delete entries and Delete App act on data stored on your device. Use the contacts in the next section for requests about any Adapty profile or Apple purchase records. To manage or cancel an Apple subscription, use your Apple Account settings.

6. Security, providers and processing locations

Local data is protected by the security features of iOS, subject to your device and backup settings. No application or service can guarantee absolute security.

We operate from Serbia. If you use Feedback, your text starts as a draft in GOODVI. You choose whether to open your mail app and send it. A draft saved in your mail app is handled by that mail service. When you send us email, our Gmail mailbox receives your sender address, subject, message and any reply address you include. We use these details to answer your question, investigate the issue you report or handle your privacy request.

Messages remain in the support mailbox until deleted. Archiving removes a conversation from the inbox and keeps it in All Mail. When a message is deleted, Gmail normally keeps it in Trash for up to 30 days before permanent deletion, unless Trash is emptied sooner. You can ask us to delete correspondence at the address above.

Adapty Tech Inc., based in the United States, processes subscription profiles on our behalf. Its Data Processing Agreement identifies its hosting providers and provides for return or deletion at our written request, when processing is no longer needed or when the agreement ends, subject to legal retention duties. Backup copies expire on a separate rotation. Apple controls its own billing records.

Apple, Google and GitHub may process information outside Serbia under the arrangements that apply to their respective services. You can ask us about where your information is processed and any safeguards that apply. Adapty’s agreement contains transfer clauses for data subject to EU, Swiss or UK law. It governs how Adapty handles data for GOODVI. Google explains how it handles data in its Privacy Policy.

When you visit this site, GitHub Pages logs your IP address for security. This site sets no tracking cookies and loads no analytics scripts. We do not collect your browsing history across other websites. This site serves the same content when your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

7. Children, changes and contact

GOODVI is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If an underage person uses GOODVI, their use and any purchase should comply with applicable law, Apple's rules and any necessary parent or guardian authorization. We do not knowingly operate a public profile or social-sharing feature for children.

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest published version. Material changes will be addressed with any notice required by law.
