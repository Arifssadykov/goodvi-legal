1. Contact us

Use Settings → Feedback when available in the app, or email arifsadykov7@gmail.com directly. Include a short description of the problem, the app version and the steps to reproduce it.

Writing in Feedback creates a draft. You decide whether to send it through your mail app. If mail is unavailable, you can copy the draft and use the address above. We receive your message only if you send it. See how support email is stored.

We keep support conversations while a request is open. We close it after our final reply, when you withdraw it, or when 30 days have passed without a reply to a question we need answered to continue. If you send a new substantive message about the same issue, we reopen the request.

Once a month we review closed GOODVI conversations, including archived ones. We move conversations that have been closed for at least 90 days to Trash. If we need particular messages for a specific dispute or a legal duty to retain them, we keep only those messages, record the reason and review it each month. At the monthly review, we move eligible messages to Trash when the reason no longer applies.

Gmail normally keeps messages in Trash for up to 30 days after we move them there, then permanently deletes them from the mailbox. They can be permanently deleted sooner if Trash is emptied. The monthly review concerns how long we keep correspondence. We handle personal data requests within the deadlines set by applicable law.

Keep passwords, one-time authentication codes, payment-card details and full private journal entries out of your message.

2. Subscription availability and management

For the U.S. App Store release, Monthly is $6.99 per month with no trial. Yearly is $34.99 per year, with a seven-day introductory trial for eligible Apple Accounts. Apple shows the applicable price and offer before purchase. To manage an active subscription billed by Apple:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad and tap your name.
  2. Select Subscriptions and then GOODVI.
  3. Use Apple's options to manage or cancel renewal.

Apple's subscription instructions. Deleting GOODVI does not stop Apple subscription billing.

3. Restore purchases or request a refund

Use Restore Purchases on GOODVI’s subscription screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Restoring a purchase does not recover deleted local journal entries.

For charges made through Apple, use reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund. Apple's decision and applicable law determine eligibility.

4. Export or remove local journal information

When your journal is accessible, you can edit individual reflections. In Settings → My data, Export entries exports all saved reflection entries as a JSON file, and Delete entries removes all saved reflection entries after you confirm. Manage exported files and saved wallpapers where you saved them.

To remove the installed application and its local app data, open Settings → General, tap iPhone Storage or iPad Storage, select GOODVI, then tap Delete App. Do not confuse this with Offload App, which retains documents and data. Apple backups must be managed separately. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Privacy and data requests

For a privacy or data request, contact arifsadykov7@gmail.com. You can ask about a GOODVI subscription profile, object to analytics or Apple Ads measurement, or request deletion of support email. We may need information to verify the request and locate the right profile. We can coordinate profile requests with Adapty. For records Apple controls, use Apple’s privacy request options. Deleting journal entries does not send a deletion request to either provider. Read about your privacy rights.

GOODVI is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple or Adapty.
